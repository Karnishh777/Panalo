The honest version

What we protect — and what we don't.

Privacy claims are easy to inflate. Here's exactly what Panalo's encryption covers.

Encrypted

  • Message text, in every chat where everyone has set up their keys
  • Photos and files you send — contents, names and sizes
  • Your searchable message history, which lives only on your device

Not hidden

  • Who you talk to, and when — encryption hides what you said, not that you said it
  • Profile photos, so people can recognise you
  • Your key can be recovered with your password — strong privacy at rest, not zero-knowledge encryption

About Panalo

Encrypted chat, built for people who like their apps to look good. Message text is encrypted on your device before it's sent, so the server stores ciphertext it can't read.

What that does and doesn't cover
  • Messages and the files you send are encrypted — in any chat where everyone has finished setting up their keys. An attachment's name and size are encrypted too, not just its contents.
  • Profile photos are not. An avatar is shown to people you share no key with — that's what it's for — so there's no key that could protect it and still let them see it.
  • Who you talk to, and when, is visible to the server. Encryption hides what you said, not that you said it.
  • Your key is recoverable from your password, which is what lets you read your history on a new device. It also means this is strong privacy at rest rather than zero-knowledge end-to-end encryption — we'd rather say so than imply otherwise.

Good to know
  • Lock a single chat from that chat's ⋮ menu — not here.
  • Hide a chat from the same ⋮ menu. Hidden chats have no switch in Settings on purpose: a visible toggle would tell anyone holding your phone that there's something to find.
  • Three separate PINs — app, chat lock, hidden chats — so one never unlocks the others.
  • PINs guard this device — they aren't encryption keys and don't protect anything on the server. What protects message text there is the chat's encryption key; see above for what it covers.
  • Calls work on most Wi-Fi. Some mobile networks block the connection type calls need.

Set a new password

Your encryption key is protected by your password. If your key is still cached on this device, we'll re-protect it with the new password automatically. If not, we'll generate fresh keys and old encrypted messages will become unreadable.

Reset your password

We'll email a reset link to your registered address. The link opens Panalo where you can pick a new one.

Change password

Your encryption key is protected by this password, so it gets re-protected with the new one automatically. Stay online until it finishes.
