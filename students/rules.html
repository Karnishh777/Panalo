Community rules

Be the kind of person you'd want in your circle.

These are the rules of the road for people using Panalo Students. They are not a legal terms-of-service document; whoever runs a Panalo deployment should publish their own terms alongside them.

Who it's for

  • Panalo Students is for students aged 13 and over.
  • One person, one account. Don't use someone else's, and don't pretend to be someone you're not.

How to treat people

  • No bullying, harassment, threats or hate. Disagree with ideas, not with people's existence.
  • No sexual content of any kind, and never anything sexual involving anyone under 18.
  • Don't share someone else's private information: their number, address, photos or messages, without their permission.
  • No spam, scams or chain messages. Don't flood rooms.
  • Don't use rooms or circles to pressure anyone into moving to another app, meeting up, or keeping secrets.
  • Hosts: let in the people who belong, and use “only hosts post” when a room needs to stay on track.

Studying together

  • Share notes and help each other — but don't share exam papers or answers you're not allowed to share, and follow your school's rules on academic honesty.
  • Only upload files you have the right to share.

When something goes wrong

  • Block anyone, any time, from their ⋯ menu. They aren't told.
  • Report a person or a message from the same menu. You can choose to include the message's text — otherwise nobody can read it, because messages are encrypted.
  • The people who run this Panalo can remove content and accounts that break these rules.
  • If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a trusted adult first. Reports aren't monitored around the clock.
