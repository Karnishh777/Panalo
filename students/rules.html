Rules

Your space, used honestly.

These are the rules for people using Panalo Students. They are not a legal terms-of-service document; whoever runs a Panalo deployment should publish their own terms alongside them.

Who it's for

  • Panalo Students is for students aged 13 and over. Under 18, a parent or guardian has to agree first.
  • One person, one account. Don't use someone else's, and don't pretend to be someone you're not.

What it's for

  • Your own studies: planning, focusing, keeping your notes and files, and looking after yourself while you do.
  • Follow your school's rules on academic honesty. Panalo is a place to do your own work.
  • Only upload files you have the right to keep.
  • Never upload anything illegal, and never anything sexual involving anyone under 18.
  • Don't try to get into anyone else's account or data, or to break or overload the service.

What happens if the rules are broken

A file can be removed, and an account suspended for a while or for good. Content that's illegal — especially anything sexual involving a minor — is removed and reported to the police.

When something goes wrong

  • Write to the people who run this Panalo from Privacy & data in the app, or email the Grievance Officer (see the privacy notes).
  • If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a trusted adult first. Panalo is not an emergency service.
