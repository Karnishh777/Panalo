Accessibility

We want Panalo to work for every student, on a school laptop as well as a phone.

  • Everything works with a keyboard, with a visible focus ring. Space starts a study session; Esc closes what's open.
  • Text and buttons are sized and coloured to stay readable; nothing depends on colour alone.
  • Motion stops when your device asks for reduced motion, and on slow laptops.
  • Pictures that carry meaning, like your world, have a description for screen readers.

Known gaps: the turning 3D world needs WebGL (a flat picture shows instead), and marking a photo of an answer needs a camera or a file. If something stops you using Panalo, tell us and we'll fix it or find another way.
