    A streak for every subject. It lights up when you're ready.

    Your subjects fly round your world as one comet. Each streak stays night-sky indigo until that subject is ready for the exam hall, then glows gold. One look tells you where you stand, without a single number.

    06 · Questions

    Questions,
    answered.

    Is it free?

    Yes. No ads, no paid tier, nothing sold.

    Which exams does it know?

    Cambridge IGCSE, AS and A Level now. ICSE and ISC are next, then CBSE.

    Do I need a phone number?

    No. An email and a username are all it asks for.

    Can other people see what I do?

    No. There are no profiles, no chat and no feed: your plan, focus and world are yours alone.

    What happens if I stop for a while?

    Nothing bad. Clouds gather over your world while you're away and clear when you come back, and the plan is rebuilt from where you are. No streaks to lose.

    Does it work on a school laptop?

    Yes, in Edge or Chrome on Windows, and it can be installed like an app.

    Can I delete everything?

    Yes, from Privacy & data: your account and your world. It can't be undone.
