    伍05 — A letter

    To whoever is reading this at an odd hour,

    You don't need another app that counts your screen time against you, or a feed that decides what you care about tonight.

    You need a quiet room, a few people you chose, a shelf for the things you made, and proof that the hours went somewhere. Every hour you focus becomes land. Every thing you finish becomes a light. Rest, and clouds roll in; come back, and they clear.

    Take your time. The universe will wait.— Panalo

    漆07 — Questions

    Questions,
    answered.

    Is it free?

    Yes. No ads, no paid tier, nothing sold.

    Do I need a phone number?

    No. You pick a username; people find you only if you tell them it, or through a room's join code.

    Who can read my messages?

    The people in the conversation. Messages are encrypted on your device before they're sent. What that does and doesn't cover.

    Who can see my files?

    Only you, unless you share one with a circle. Archive files aren't end-to-end encrypted, so we say so.

    What happens if I stop for a while?

    Nothing bad. Clouds gather over your world while you're away and clear when you come back. No streaks to lose.

    Can I delete everything?

    Yes, from Safety & privacy: your account, every message you sent, every file you uploaded and your world. It can't be undone.
