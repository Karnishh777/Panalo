    伍05 — A letter

    To whoever is reading this at an odd hour,

    You don't need another app that counts your screen time against you, or a feed that decides what you care about tonight.

    You need a quiet room, a plan you can trust, a shelf for the things you made, and proof that the hours went somewhere. Every hour you focus becomes land. Every thing you finish becomes a light. Rest, and clouds roll in; come back, and they clear.

    Take your time. The universe will wait.— Panalo

    漆07 — Questions

    Questions,
    answered.

    Is it free?

    Yes. No ads, no paid tier, nothing sold.

    Do I need a phone number?

    No. An email and a username are all it asks for.

    Can other people see what I do?

    No. There are no profiles, no chat and no feed: your tasks, focus, files and world are yours alone.

    Who can see my files?

    Only you. Archive files aren't end-to-end encrypted, so we say so.

    What happens if I stop for a while?

    Nothing bad. Clouds gather over your world while you're away and clear when you come back. No streaks to lose.

    Can I delete everything?

    Yes, from Privacy & data: your account, every file you uploaded and your world. It can't be undone.
